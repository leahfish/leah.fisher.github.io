Coalition Partner Intelligence Tracker

Penn Quarter Partners · Healthcare Policy & Research Intern · May 2025 – Aug 2025

Situation

A healthcare coalition client needed visibility into what its 28 partner organizations were doing each month. Partner activity lived scattered across 28 separate websites and social media accounts, with no central record, which meant the client had no practical way to see the coalition’s collective footprint or to spot developments worth acting on.

Task

I was asked to build a monthly digest capturing every partner’s events, press releases, announcements, and news, and to deliver it in a format the client could scan quickly and use as a reference rather than read once and discard.

Action

I established a repeatable process for working through all 28 partners: reviewing each organization’s website and social channels, identifying what was new since the prior month, and sorting findings into consistent categories so entries stayed comparable across partners. I hyperlinked every item back to its source so anyone reading could verify or go deeper without repeating the search, and organized the document so a reader could navigate by partner or scan the full month at a glance. After the first month, I refined the structure based on how the output was actually being used and ran the process again the following month.

Result

The client gained a single monthly reference covering coalition-wide activity that had previously required 28 separate lookups. The format held up across two cycles, and building it as a repeatable process rather than a one-time research task meant subsequent months took less time while producing consistent, comparable output.
