Client Service Issue Analysis

Kalos Golf · Operations & Client Services Intern · Jan 2024 - Dec 2024

Situation

As an Operations & Client Services Intern at Kalos Golf, I worked closely with the sales, travel, and operations teams supporting luxury international golf trips.

Task

Leadership wanted to understand why certain client service issues kept recurring so they could be addressed proactively.

Action

I analyzed client survey data to isolate recurring service issues, presenting patterns and solutions that informed management decisions, while coordinating across teams to execute travel logistics.

Result

The analysis gave management clear, evidence-based patterns to act on, and the cross-team coordination helped ensure timely client communications throughout international trips.
