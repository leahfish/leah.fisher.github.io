AI Adoption & Reporting Survey Design

Penn Quarter Partners · Healthcare Policy & Research Intern · May 2025 - Aug 2025

Situation

Leadership at the same biopharma trade association wanted to understand how member organizations were adopting AI and how well existing financial reporting practices were working, while also preparing for the firm’s annual meeting.

Task

I was asked to design instruments that would capture this information at scale, and to help deliver clear communications around the results and the annual meeting itself.

Action

I designed Google Forms survey instruments measuring firm-wide AI adoption and streamlined financial reporting for senior partners, and authored social media toolkits, press releases, and multi-platform copy supporting client report releases and campaigns. I also orchestrated the annual meeting end-to-end: building the agenda and run-of-show, drafting executive remarks, preparing briefing materials, and directing day-of vendor logistics.

Result

The surveys and communications materials gave senior partners a clear, data-backed view of adoption trends to share with clients and members, and the annual meeting was delivered as an on-time, well-run program.
