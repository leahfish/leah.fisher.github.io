Annual Meeting Planning & Execution

Penn Quarter Partners · Healthcare Policy & Research Intern · May 2025 – Aug 2025

Situation

A national biopharma trade association holds an annual policy meeting that brings together member executives, advocates, and stakeholders. Planning spans months and runs across several workstreams at once, from speaker preparation to attendee materials to on-site logistics.

Task

I was responsible for a set of deliverables supporting the meeting: preparing materials on attendees, drafting remarks for the executive opening and closing the program, coordinating the advocate video component, and keeping the master project tracker current so the team could see status across every workstream.

Action

I built a document profiling every luncheon attendee, researching each person’s background in the healthcare environment so leadership could speak to the room knowledgeably. I drafted the first version of the executive’s introduction and closing remarks. For the advocate video, I wrote the outreach emails inviting advocates to participate, then scheduled and recorded the interview sessions, and contributed to the storyboard that assembled soundbites, on-screen text, and sourcing. I also researched and sourced vendor options for printed event materials, and maintained the project tracker that the team worked from.

Result

The meeting ran on schedule with materials in place. The attendee profiles and executive remarks let leadership engage the room with specific context, and the advocate interviews produced footage used in the association’s board video.
