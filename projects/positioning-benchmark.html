Healthcare Industry Positioning Benchmark

Penn Quarter Partners · Healthcare Policy & Research Intern · May 2025 - Aug 2025

Situation

As a Healthcare Policy & Research Intern at Penn Quarter Partners, a policy and communications firm in Washington, D.C., I supported the team’s work for a national biopharma trade association.

Task

The association needed a clear picture of how peer consulting firms were positioned in the market to help inform its own strategic direction.

Action

I benchmarked peer consulting firms across their mission statements, organizational structures, and core capabilities.

Result

The comparative analysis directly informed the firm’s internal strategic positioning discussions with senior partners.
